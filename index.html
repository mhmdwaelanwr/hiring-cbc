BEFORE YOU APPLY

A few useful answers.

The application is designed to understand fit, commitment and evidence — not to reward fancy titles.

Do I need previous club experience?

No. Relevant experience helps, but projects, volunteering, freelance work, coursework and things you independently shipped can all demonstrate ability.

Can I apply to more than one team?

Yes. The application asks for a first and second team preference, with a preferred role for each.

Are these only leadership positions?

No. The recruitment includes leads, coordinators, specialists, members and volunteer paths across technical and non-technical teams.

Will I lose my application if I close the page?

Your draft is saved locally in your browser while you type. You can return on the same device and continue before submitting.
